Delicious Chocolate Cake Recipe:
Ingredients: 2 cups all-purpose flour, 2 cups sugar, 3/4 cup cocoa powder, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp salt, 2 eggs, 1 cup milk, 1/2 cup vegetable oil, 2 tsp vanilla extract.
Instructions: Preheat oven to 350F (175C). Grease two 9-inch round baking pans. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Beat for 2 minutes. Stir in 1 cup boiling water. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool before frosting.
